📖 How to Train the Model

  1. Prepare your dataset: Ensure you have annotated your meter images using the standard YOLO format on your computer.
  2. Locate the Config: Find your data.yaml file. This is a simple configuration file that tells our system where your images and labels are located.
  3. Upload: Click the upload box below and select that data.yaml configuration file.
  4. Start Training: Click the "Start YOLO Training" button.
  5. Monitor: Watch the log console at the bottom of the screen to view the progress in real-time as the AI learns how to spot the meters.

Warning: YOLO computer vision training is highly intensive. Please ensure you leave this terminal running without interruption.
