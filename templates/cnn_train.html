📖 How to Train the Model

  1. Prepare your data: Ensure all your cropped rotary numbers are organized into folders named after their number (e.g., a folder named 0 containing all '0' images, 1 for '1' images, up to 9).
  2. Package it: Put all those numbered folders inside one main folder (e.g., rotary_dataset).
  3. Upload: Click the upload area below and select the main folder you just created.
  4. Train: Click the "Start Deep Learning Training" button. The server will automatically process your images, learn the numbers, and test itself.
  5. Wait: Watch the log console for progress. When it says the training is complete, a result image will appear showing what it learned!

Note: The training might take a few minutes depending on your computer speed. Don't close the server while it's running!
